What is Phishing Defence Platform?

Phishing Defence is an educational platform designed to help users identify and protect themselves against phishing attacks. Through interactive simulations and training scenarios, users will develop awareness of how social engineering works and learn how to spot malicious content.

Key Features

• Realistic phishing simulations
• Immediate feedback and learning tips
• Visual and interactive learning experience
• Developed with cybersecurity standards in mind (ISO 27001, GDPR compliance)
